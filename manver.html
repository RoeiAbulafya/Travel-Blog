My Travels

Manchester & Verona

This trip was a long time coming. My best friend (who is incidentally also my cousin) moved to Manchester with his wife and they had a kid there that I had to meet. Manchester exceeded my expectations. It is such a beautiful city, ancient and vibrant place. We also went to York and Chster which solidified my love to the UK, even though it was my first time there. When I was looking at flights, I saw a connection in Verona, north Italy. You already know I love Italy so I seized the opportunity and got a hotel in there for 5 days. Damn it I love Italy. I took a private pasta class from a nice Italian couple and had a great time learning about all the pasta shapes and the Italian cuisine. I will return to Manchester to see my cousin and I will return to Verona to learn about gnocchi this time.

Picturesque Moments

Favourite attractions:

  • City Centre of Machester:

    The minute I arrived in Manchester airport, my bag ripped apart. I was pissed. But, the very next day I went to the city centre to Arndale shopping centre and bought me a new one in TKmaxx. The mall is one of the boggest Iv'e ever seen and I bought so many things it was ripping my credit card in half. Outside of the mall, there's the Market Street which is a great street with even more shops and vendors, great food stands and street performances. I sat in Piccadilly Gardens, eating a red velvet cake enjoying the culture and the great vibes this city offers.

  • York:

    Only two hours drive (on the left side!) there's York. The original one. This place has ancient vibes, Like the 9th roman legion is still there. The houses are from different eras and it shows, the church of York is Gothic and magnificent and the beer is better than any beer I tasted ever. There are of course, many museums and souveneir shops so it is the perfect place for the history nerds! (such as my cousin and I). There's also a great flea market- Shambles- if you like niche alcohol, this is your place to be.

  • Chester:

    If you thought York was ancient, wait till you see Chester. Called after the deceased lead singer of Linkin Park, Chester is an incredible city with a great history. It also has a massive cathedral and a nice shopping centre. Sit in a pub, grab a pint of ale and absorb the vibes. I promise you, It's a life changing place.

  • Verona:

    A weird shift from the English vibes back to Italy. The most famous story, Romeo and Juliet happened here, in this romantic city. There's even a statue of Juliet and they say if you touch her breasts and you are single, you will have relationship. I did not yield to the pressure and maybe that's why I'm still single. Anyway, the city itself is so fun, with the river flowing across, the great bridges and towers, the great people and most importantly, the food.

Travel Tips:

  • Food in Manchester: When you walk around the mall, you might get hungry and want to eat something. Don't order anything inside the mall (except from the cake stand, get the red velvet). Go outside, near Piccadilly Gardens there are some great food trucks from all around the world and the smell in intoxicating.
  • Harry Potter:If your'e a fan, or someone you know is, get inside one of the many Harry Potter souvenier stores in the UK. They seem to be very proud of that and there's this great shop in York that sells the greatest merchandise. Do yourself a favour and at least get a scarf with your Hogwarts house's colors.
  • Markets: You can shop in the mall, but there are so many great markets, in every city in this trip and it is so much cheaper than any mall store. Whether it is souveneir, food or clothes store, the markets are usually cheaper, better and supports local businesses.
  • Go to a cooking class: I love pasta and Iv'e always wanted to make some on my own. So I ordered a cooking class through TripAdvisor and stumbled upon this class in Verona. I signed up but the day before the class I got a message that many has cancelled and they want me to move it but I couldn't. The original evening was my last in Verona so they kept it in the same date and I had a private class. Dave and Valli was great hosts and they were so nice! They taught me all this kinds of pasta shapes and how to make it from scratch. We even made ravioli and tiramisu! It was such a lovely evening, the food was great of course and I was over the moon.
  • Hotel Locations: It's a recurring theme in all my travels. I want a good location to sleep in to be close to the center but I don't want to pay expansive prices on crappy hotels. My takeaway is that the perfect hotel is 10 minutes walk from the center. It will be cheaper and quieter. You'll have to walk a bit but you already eat too much on vacation so you know, it evens out. Don't worry, If your'e laxy, there are great metros and trams all around the world so you don't have to wal as much.
